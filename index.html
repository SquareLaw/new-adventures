How we work

Phase 1

Quick 30 mins chat to understand your needs. Then a longer discussion where you explain your pain point, your flows, your stack.

Phase 2

We quote you for the project. We collect data from you: templates, laws, playbooks (no clients data exposure).

Phase 3

We create a MVP you can test. Test and debug, iterations until happy.

Phase 4

We deploy the finished software on your servers.

How much it costs

It depends. 2000-6000 Euros is the range you may look at, with bigger quotes for bigger projects. You need to factor in the costs for servers, Claude API, or other tools if needed.

Isn't it easier or cheaper to buy a SaaS?

It depends. On a few factors: how complex is the task you need to take care of, how many users you need, if customisation is required.

Will AI hallucinate?

Still possible but the event is greatly reduced with RAG and with clear indication of the sources.

Do all your builds require AI?

No, for instance document automation doesn't.

How long does a project take?

Depends on scope, same as the price. A simple automation can be live in a couple of weeks; bigger builds take longer.

What if I don't like the MVP?

That's what the fixing phase is for. We adjust it until it actually works for you.

Who owns the code once it's built?

You do. It runs on your own servers, not ours.
